# EXPERIENCED


## 1. Introduction

So, you are an expert? Well, you are at the right place! Being an experienced programmer, you can contribute to project ***Tosbit*** through profiling performance, writing complex algorithms (as in documentation) & guiding fellow beginners or intermediates. My team warmly welcomes experienced programmers to this project, its an honour!
