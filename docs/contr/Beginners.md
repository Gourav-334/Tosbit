# BEGINNERS


## 1. Introduction

For beginners, who are here due to curiosity & want to play around with ***Tosbit***, you are most welcome! Most of these tasks require minimal understanding of C programming & not much about theory. So, most beginners can spend healthy time to read documentation & contribute to this project through what are listed in this file.


## 2. Terminologies

- **<u>Code breaking</u>:** Finding unexpected errors in command execution with right syntax.
